How the levels were set

  • N4: everyday things a learner meets early. Food (野菜 and 果物 are N5 on the lists; the official draft has うどん at N5), shops and places (コンビニ, 駅前), simple loanwords (カフェ, アイス, バイト).
  • N3: everyday life, school, work and notices. Computer and phone words people use (動画, ダウンロード, パスワード, 返信), job and place words (市長, 店内, 担任), pairs of words already at N3 (最小 with 最大, 北部 and 西部 with 南部, 次回 and 毎回 with 今回).
  • N2: articles, news and work on general topics (虐待, 被災, 円高, 転職), business and technical-but-common words (手数料, 端末, セキュリティー), and pairs already at N2 (戦前 with 戦後, 中期 and 前期 with 初期).
  • N1: editorials, abstract and specialised news: politics, law, finance, medicine, science, history and military (懸念, 示唆, 破綻, 臨床, 大名, 艦隊).

Polite お / ご forms

Listed when the お-form is how people say the word, as the lists already do (お茶, お金 and お風呂 at N5, お土産 at N4, お互い at N3, with the bare word separately). So お願い, お湯 and お世話 go to N4 and お尻 to N3. ご協力 is skipped, because 協力 is mostly used without ご. A card for either form counts toward both.
